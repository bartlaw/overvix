Spot-Vol & Pearson Correlation Playbook

The 4 Market Regimes: Combining Overvix Residuals with Dynamic Pearson Correlation

Two Independent Dimensions:

Pearson Correlation (ρ) measures Directional Synchronicity (Are SPX & VIX moving opposite or decoupling?).
The Overvix Signal measures Magnitude Excess (Did VIX overreact by ≥ +2.0 pts relative to the size of the SPX move?).

Slope: β = ρ × (σVIX / σSPX)
Model Fit: R² = ρ²
Regime Pearson Correlation (ρ) Overvix Signal Market Reality & Trading Interpretation
1. Classic Capitulation
(Orderly Panic) 		Strongly Inverse

ρ ≈ -0.75 to -0.90

 🚨 OVERVIXED

≥ +2.0 pts

 Panic Overreaction: SPX is dumping, and dealers are getting squeezed into buying puts with extreme magnitude. High-probability mean-reversion long setup once selling volume slows.
2. Bullish Decoupling
(Hedging / Squeeze) 		Decoupled / Positive

ρ > -0.20 or ρ > 0

 🚨 OVERVIXED

≥ +2.0 pts

 Structural Anomaly: SPX is flat or rising, but VIX is spiking anyway. Institutional traders are aggressively paying up for upside calls or buying tail-risk crash puts ahead of a major binary event (e.g., FOMC, CPI).
3. Healthy Orderly Market Normal Inverse

ρ ≈ -0.70 to -0.85

 NORMAL

-1.0 to +1.0 pts

 Equilibrium: Normal market maker delta-hedging. VIX moves smoothly along the regression trendline in orderly fashion.
4. Volatility Crush
/ Exhaustion 		Decoupled / Weak

ρ > -0.30

 UNDER-VIXED

Negative Residual

 Fear Deflation: SPX drops, but VIX refuses to rise (or drops). Option sellers dominate; fear is completely depleted. Often marks a market bottom.
