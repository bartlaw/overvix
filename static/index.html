Quantitative Formulation: Pearson Correlation ρ(Δln St, Δln Volt)

Regime Indicator • Never a Hedge Ratio
1. Strict Return Stationarity

Always computed on log returns (Δln St vs. Δln VIXt) or percentage changes, never on raw index levels (which introduces spurious non-stationary correlation).

2. Typical Equilibrium Band (-0.70 to -0.85)

A standard 20-day or 60-day rolling Pearson correlation naturally oscillates between -0.70 and -0.85 under normal dealer gamma hedging conditions.

3. Regime Shift Trigger (ρ > -0.50)

When ρ breaks above -0.50 or turns positive (ρ > 0), it is not a hedge adjustment signal, but an alert for vol-of-vol stress, call-buying frenzies, or dealer repositioning.
